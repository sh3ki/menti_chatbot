Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us.

MENTI is an academic project designed to explore how artificial intelligence and emotion-aware technology can support mental health and emotional well-being. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, and protected when users interact with the MENTI system.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features used, MENTI may process information provided by users or generated through interaction with the system.

Personal Information

Name, email address, account credentials, or identifying details may be collected when registering or authenticating.

Conversation, Emotion, and Assessment Data

Messages, conversational inputs, emotion-related information, and questionnaire responses may be processed to provide chatbot interactions, emotion-aware features, and well-being views.

Technical Information

Essential technical metadata, including sessions, authentication tokens, and browser compatibility information, may be processed for normal operations.

2. How We Use Information

Information is used to provide and maintain MENTI, generate responses, support emotion-aware functionality, provide assessment and progress features, improve usability, maintain security, address technical issues, and support the academic evaluation of the thesis project where applicable and permitted.

3. AI and Conversational Data

Information in chatbot interactions may be processed by the underlying AI service used to generate responses. Users should avoid entering highly sensitive information, such as financial records, government ID numbers, or unnecessary personal identification.

4. Emotion-Aware Processing

Emotion-related information supports contextually appropriate responses, emotional pattern awareness, progress tracking, and other emotion-aware features. Emotion detection is not a medical diagnosis.

5. Psychological Assessments

Questionnaire-based assessments are intended for self-reflection and informational well-being views. Results are not medical or psychiatric diagnoses and do not replace professional evaluation.

6. Data Storage and Retention

Information is stored only as long as necessary to provide functionality, maintain system records, or fulfill legitimate academic and technical requirements.

Storage Policy: Standard Active Project Lifecycle

7. Data Security

MENTI applies reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or loss, including secure authentication, access controls, protected API communication, and role-based permissions.

8. Data Sharing and Third Parties

MENTI does not sell users' personal information. Information may be shared with service infrastructure only when necessary for functionality, such as cloud databases or AI endpoint services required for text processing.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable regulations and system scope, users may request access, correction, or deletion of account records; inquire about processing; and withdraw from optional features where technically supported.

10. Children's Privacy

MENTI is not intentionally designed to collect personal information from children without appropriate authorization or adult guidance. Users who cannot legally use the service independently should access it only with proper consent and supervision.

11. Cookies and Tracking

MENTI limits tracking to essential cookies and local storage required for logins, session safety, and core navigation. It does not employ third-party advertising trackers, cross-site profiling cookies, or marketing analytics pixels.

12. Mental Health Disclaimer

MENTI is an AI-based support system intended to encourage emotional awareness, self-reflection, and supportive conversation. It does not diagnose mental health conditions or replace psychologists, psychiatrists, counselors, doctors, emergency services, or other qualified professionals. AI-generated responses may not always be accurate or appropriate.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as MENTI's features, implementation, or academic requirements evolve.

Last Updated: September 2026
