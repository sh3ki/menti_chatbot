About The Project

Meet MENTI

An Emotion-Aware Chatbot System for Mental Health Support

MENTI is an AI-powered support system designed to provide users with a safe, supportive, and accessible space to express their thoughts and emotions. Through emotion-aware technology and empathetic conversations, MENTI aims to encourage self-reflection, emotional awareness, and healthier ways of understanding one's feelings.

Why MENTI?

Mental health can be difficult to talk about. Sometimes, people may struggle to express what they are feeling, understand their emotions, or find a comfortable space to simply talk.

MENTI was created to explore how technology can provide accessible emotional support through artificial intelligence. By combining empathetic conversations, emotion awareness, and progress tracking, MENTI aims to help users better understand their emotional experiences and encourage self-reflection.

We believe that technology can be more than just functional—it can also be designed with empathy.

Technology with Empathy

MENTI is built around the belief that technology should not only provide answers—it should also listen.

Our approach focuses on creating an experience that is supportive, respectful, emotionally aware, and non-judgmental. MENTI is designed to recognize emotional context and respond with empathy while encouraging healthy self-reflection.

Artificial intelligence can provide support, but it cannot replace the expertise, understanding, and care provided by qualified mental health professionals.

Our Mission

“To make emotional support more accessible through thoughtful technology while encouraging self-awareness, emotional reflection, and connection to appropriate human support.”

Our goal is to explore how artificial intelligence can contribute to a more accessible and supportive digital environment while maintaining responsibility, empathy, and respect for the user's well-being.

Important Notice

MENTI is an AI-based mental health support system designed to encourage emotional awareness, self-reflection, and supportive conversations.

MENTI does not diagnose mental health conditions and is not a replacement for psychologists, psychiatrists, counselors, doctors, emergency services, or other qualified healthcare professionals. If you are experiencing a mental health emergency, please contact appropriate emergency services, a qualified healthcare professional, or a trusted person who can provide immediate support.
